# Clinician review of the items

**Reviewer:** Maryam Khalid, physiotherapist (Doctor of Physical Therapy), November 2026, after the model runs. She is married to the author.

**What she checked:** the 48 items of name set A in all three forms (English, Roman Urdu, chat spelling) against the clinic records, one row per item, Y/N for four questions, plus a comment in her own words. The sheet showed the expected answer. Name set B (same numbers, other names) was not reviewed.

- **Not natural (7):** all gender agreement. A09 to A11 (T03, age in N years) use *honge* for female patients, where *hongi* is correct; A33 to A36 (T09, clinic days) use *aate hain* for female therapists, where *aati hain* is correct.
- **Not realistic (16):** every item of T03 to T06 (A09 to A24): age in N years, age N years ago, combined and difference in therapists' experience. Her comments: a receptionist or physiotherapist does not ask these.
- The items were not changed after the review, so all reported results use the original wording.
